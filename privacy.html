Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This policy applies to Redline Studio BD’s website and mobile games, including Road Fight, Shipment Master, and Ice Crusher.

Information we process

Our games may process gameplay progress, settings, device and app information, advertising identifiers, crash diagnostics, analytics events, and purchase-related status needed to operate, improve, secure, and monetize the games. Our website host may process basic access information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and access time.

How information is used

  • Operate games and save progress or settings
  • Provide advertising and measure performance
  • Process and restore eligible in-app purchases
  • Diagnose crashes, prevent fraud, and improve stability
  • Respond to player support requests
  • Comply with applicable platform and legal requirements

Third-party services

Depending on the game and version, our games may use services supplied by Google Play, Firebase, Unity, Unity LevelPlay, and advertising mediation partners. These providers process data under their own privacy policies. Purchases are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store full payment-card details.
