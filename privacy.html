Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RedLine Studio BD handles privacy for our mobile games and website.

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Overview

RedLine Studio BD creates mobile games for Android. We respect player privacy and aim to keep our games simple, safe, and transparent.

Information we collect

Our games may collect basic technical information needed for app functionality, analytics, crash reports, or advertising services, depending on the version of the game.

Advertising

Some games may show ads. Advertising partners may use device identifiers and related data to deliver, limit, and measure ads according to their own privacy policies.

Children

Our games are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

Our games may use third-party services such as Google Play services, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising networks. These services may collect information according to their own policies.
